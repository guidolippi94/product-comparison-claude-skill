# Intake questions

Golden rule: few questions, closed options, never ones you can already answer. The fixed questions live in SKILL.md; here you pick 1-2 category-specific ones. If the category is missing, derive them from: "which characteristic would change the winner from one user to another?".

## Electronics and computing

- Ecosystem already owned (Apple, Android, Windows, Google Home, etc.)? Decides compatibility.
- Main use (work, gaming, creative, study)? Changes performance criteria.
- Portability or power?

## Appliances and home

- Available space and dimensions, energy class wanted, tolerable noise.
- Number of people, pets or children.
- Installation: needed, or standalone?

## Health, sport, nutrition, personal care

- Precise goal and experience level.
- Allergies, intolerances, relevant medical conditions (if declared, use them for the research; do not store them or repeat them in the sheet beyond what is necessary).
- For supplements and products that affect health: look for studies, check regulators for the market (EFSA in the EU, FDA/NIH in the US, MHRA in the UK), and remember you do not replace medical advice.

## Cars, motorbikes, bikes, mobility

- Annual distance and type of route, total cost of ownership (not just purchase), charging or garage availability.

## Clothing, shoes, outdoor

- Size or foot shape, use (daily, trekking, running), climate, importance of materials and sustainability.

## Software, subscriptions, services

- Number of users, must-have integrations, time horizon (months or years), sensitivity to lock-in and data.

## Kitchen, food, household care

- Frequency of use, ease of cleaning, capacity, budget for consumables.

## Phrasing questions

Prefer closed choices with a recommended option, in the user's currency:

- "Budget: up to X / X to Y / above Y / flexible if worth it".
- "Main priority: performance / durability / price / ease of use".

Always leave room for a free answer ("other"). If the user answers "I don't know", pick a reasonable default, state it in the recap and move on.
